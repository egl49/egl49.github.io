## Research

<div class="research-card">

<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Numerical Relativity & Compact Objects</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Investigating the tidal deformability of neutron stars using numerical relativity tools. This work utilizes the SpECTRE code base to simulate binary neutron star systems and compare how differing internal equations of state alter gravitational wave observables, aiming to connect microscopic nuclear physics with macroscopic signals detectable by next-generation gravitational wave observatories.</p>
</div>
</div>

<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/nebula.svg" class="research-thumb" alt="Dumbbell Nebula M27">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Observational Astronomy & Nebular Evolution</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Analyzing long-baseline evolutionary characteristics of planetary nebulae. By leveraging over a century of archival and modern observations of the Dumbbell Nebula (M27), this research calculates precise expansion rates and structural dynamics over a 119-year baseline to better constrain late-stage stellar evolution models.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/instrumentation.svg" class="research-thumb" alt="TIME Instrument detector">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Astronomical Instrumentation</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Developing and optimizing experimental submillimeter spectrometers for cosmological surveys. This work includes the hands-on laboratory characterization, hardware calibration, and optical optimization of the TIME instrument to enhance line-intensity mapping performance for studying the Epoch of Reionization.</p>
</div>
